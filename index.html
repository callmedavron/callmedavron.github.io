FlowBreak — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data Collection

FlowBreak does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or information about the websites you visit.

Local Storage

All data saved by the extension (timer settings and rest time statistics) is stored exclusively on your local device using chrome.storage.local and never leaves your device.

Third-party Services

The extension does not use analytics, advertising networks, or any third-party services. No network requests are made to external servers.
