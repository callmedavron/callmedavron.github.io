FlowBreak — Privacy Policy FlowBreak does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or information about the websites you visit. All data saved by the extension (your timer settings and rest time statistics) is stored exclusively on your local device using the browser's built-in storage API (chrome.storage.local) and never leaves your device. The extension does not use analytics, advertising networks, or any third-party services. The extension does not make any network requests to external servers. Last updated: March 2026
